Executive Summary & Breakthrough Findings

Order Flow Auctions (OFA) and Proposer-Builder Separation (PBS) have fundamentally altered the landscape of decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidations. This open-science research package provides the complete, machine-audited dataset and companion monograph analyzing 1,157 on-chain liquidations across Ethereum L1, Base L2, and Arbitrum during the severe market volatility regime of October 12–19, 2026.

By deploying high-throughput WebSocket telemetry monitoring 83 Morpho Blue markets (over 280,000 flashblocks and 5.6M+ transactions decoded) coupled with atomic multi-call receipt decoders, this work presents the first independent empirical proof of Oracle Extractable Value (OEV) recapture under live market stress:

  • 1. 98.10% Protocol Value Recapture: Across 105 verified Chainlink Smart Value Recapture (SVR) liquidations on Base Morpho Blue and Aave v3 totaling $190,506.31 in gross liquidated collateral, competitive solver bidding redirected $186,889.85 USD (98.10%) directly back to protocol reserves and depositors as competitive bids.
  • 2. Bertrand Margin Compression & Winner's Curse: Unconstrained price competition among permissionless solvers compressed gross margins to 1.86% ($3,545.73 USD pre-swap), with median margin settling at -1.55%. In 61.9% of transactions (65/105), solvers concluded at zero or negative net margin prior to off-chain DEX slippage. We document severe overbidding events (e.g. Tx 0x76a4c8...) where solvers bid up to 102.79% of collateral value, absorbing out-of-pocket losses of -$522.48 to win transaction inclusion.
  • 3. Decentralization & Absence of Monopolistic Capture: Active participation from 20 distinct solver contract architectures and 27 signing EOAs confirms that order flow auctions maintain genuine Bertrand decentralization without cartelization. While solver 0x8b61... won 48.6% of transaction counts by dominating micro-liquidations, its volume share was capped at 31.64%, with independent solvers (0x3ae5..., 0xbd0d..., 0xd128...) driving competitive pressure on large liquidations.
  • 4. Multi-Call Bundling Complexity (Methodological Advance): 64.76% (68/105) of SVR transactions were atomic multi-calls executing up to 32 liquidation sub-calls in a single transaction (Tx 0xc0a519..., $20,969.08 gross). We demonstrate that naive single-event indexers miscalculate liquidation volume and solver PnL by failing to parse recursive sub-call execution trees.
  • 5. Ethereum L1 Power-Law Distribution: Analysis of 1,049 Ethereum L1 liquidations reveals that 80.84% (848 txs) of MEV-Boost block builder priority payments are micro-dust (< $1.00 USD, median $0.08 USD), while the top 22 whale liquidations capture 96.05% ($344,155.62 USD) of all builder revenue via private PBS bundles.
