### 6. Abstract (İngilizce Özet)

```text
Order Flow Auctions (OFA) and Proposer-Builder Separation (PBS) have fundamentally altered the landscape of decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidations. This empirical study provides an exhaustive on-chain microstructure audit of 1,157 verified liquidations across Ethereum L1, Base L2, and Arbitrum recorded during the acute market volatility regime of October 12–19, 2026. 

Across 105 multi-call verified Chainlink Smart Value Recapture (SVR) transactions on Base (Morpho Blue and Aave v3) totaling $190,506.31 in gross liquidated collateral, we present the first independent empirical proof that competitive solver bidding redirects 98.10% ($186,889.85 USD) of gross liquidation value directly back to lending protocols and depositors as bids. Unconstrained Bertrand price competition among permissionless solvers compresses gross margins to 1.86% ($3,545.73 USD pre-swap), with median transaction margins settling at -1.55%. Over 61.9% of transactions (65/105) concluded at zero or negative net profit prior to off-chain DEX slippage. We identify active participation from 20 distinct solver contract architectures and 27 signing EOAs, refuting monopolistic cartelization (top solver volume share capped at 31.64%). Furthermore, 64.76% of SVR transactions were atomic multi-call bundles executing up to 32 liquidation sub-calls in a single transaction, exposing severe undercounting biases in naive single-event indexers. 

In contrast, analysis of 1,049 Ethereum L1 liquidations reveals an extreme power-law distribution where 80.84% (848 txs) of MEV-Boost block builder priority fees are micro-dust (< $1.00 USD, median $0.08 USD), while the top 22 whale liquidations capture 96.05% ($344,155.62 USD) of all builder revenue via private PBS bundles. 

All underlying empirical datasets, machine-audited receipts, and verification scripts are openly accessible under CERN Zenodo Concept DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23623588 and mirrored on Hugging Face Datasets (pCwOrM/werracle-oev-liquidation-dataset-2026).
```
