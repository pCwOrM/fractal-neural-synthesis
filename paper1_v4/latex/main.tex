\begin{abstract}
Conventional neural networks store synaptic parameters as explicit tensors whose persistent Flash footprint scales linearly ($O(W)$) with parameter count, constraining resource-limited edge microcontrollers. We formulate and systematically evaluate \textbf{Fractal Neural Synthesis (FNS)}, a procedural weight derivation architecture that replaces hidden-layer matrices with a constant $24\text{-byte}$ coordinate seed $\Theta = (c_x, c_y, \ell) \in \mathbb{F}_{64}^3$ anchored near the complex quadratic bifurcation boundary $\partial\mathcal{M}$ of $f_c(z)=z^2+c$. We introduce a large-bailout ($R=256$) smooth escape kernel, four zero-overhead geometric sampling apertures---Cartesian Grid, coprime $3\times 3$ Rule-of-Thirds ($\mathbb{Z}/9\mathbb{Z}$), $\phi$-Vogel spiral, and multi-scale Harmonic Tripod---and prove conditional Kolmogorov ($K_{\mathrm{Kolm}} \le 192+c_0$ bits) and Shannon entropy bounds. Across magnifications $\zeta\in[10^0,10^8]$, $C^1$ Fourier and lattice-noise bases collapse to post-standardization rank $\approx 2.25$ and SHA-256 PRNG exhibits avalanche discontinuity ($\rho\approx 0.019$, $50.6\%$ micro-step retention), whereas $\partial\mathcal{M}$ uniquely sustains both rank ($\approx 10.0$) and local continuity ($\rho\in[0.81,0.97]$, $89.2\%$ retention). Under leak-free 10-fold CV across four zero-scaler-byte non-linear benchmarks, FNS with an INT8 readout surpasses $0\text{-hidden}$ Linear-INT8 by $+3.6$ to $+10.8$ pp while delivering $10.7\times$--$129\times$ total Flash reduction ($725\times$--$2{,}773\times$ hidden reduction) relative to converged Float32 MLPs ($90.30\%$ Ionosphere in $285\text{ B}$; $97.22\%$ Optical Digits in $2{,}598\text{ B}$; $97.90\%$ PenDigits in $2{,}598\text{ B}$; $87.00\%$ Satimage in $1{,}570\text{ B}$).
\end{abstract}
